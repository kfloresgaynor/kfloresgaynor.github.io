Disclaimers and scope

  • This is a simplified historical and editorial visualization, not legal advice or a case-specific legal conclusion.
  • It assumes an adult victim, an offense completed on the selected date, and no tolling, delayed accrual, avoidance of prosecution, pending prosecution, corpus-delicti discovery rule, victim-age rule, or other exception.
  • The yellow ranges are offense-date eligibility ranges for later amendments; they are not independent prosecution windows.
  • The DNA provision is conditional, not automatic. Its actual deadline depends on when a qualifying DNA record is determined to match the DNA record of an identifiable person.
  • The interactive five-year DNA segment assumes the qualifying DNA determination occurs on the base 25-year deadline. A different determination date can produce a different result.
  • The visualization intentionally excludes later amendments not included in the project brief. Calendar-year anniversary calculations are used for display.
